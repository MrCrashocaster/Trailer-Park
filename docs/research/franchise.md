# Franchise and chronology

Status: recalled background, not externally verified in this task. Dates below are provisional until checked against release records. Spoilers throughout the library.

## Scope

Mike Clattenburg created the Canadian mockumentary series. Its central setting is fictional Sunnyvale Trailer Park in Nova Scotia. Robb Wells plays Ricky, John Paul Tremblay plays Julian, and Mike Smith plays Bubbles. John Dunsworth plays Jim Lahey; Patrick Roach plays Randy.

The central premise combines small-scale money-making schemes, friendship, family friction, park politics, and recurring encounters with police and jail. The protagonists are not conventional successful criminals: their goals are often immediate comfort or financial breathing room, and their plans repeatedly undermine those goals.
